Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

You can reset your password from the Login page by clicking "Forgot Password".

How can I block my debit card?

Go to Cards → Manage Cards → Block Card or contact customer support.

Why is my transfer pending?

Transfers may take a few minutes depending on the payment network.
